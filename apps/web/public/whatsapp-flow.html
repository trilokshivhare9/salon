Why the Bot Said "Expired Option" to "Hi" Earlier

In the previous code, the stale button validation ran before checking if the message was "Hi".

The Fix: Greetings (hi, hello, menu, start) are now checked at Line 1 of the handler. A customer sending "Hi" will NEVER be told their message is an expired option again!
